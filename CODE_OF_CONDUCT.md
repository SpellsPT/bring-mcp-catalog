# Code of Conduct

This is a small project. The expectations are short.

**Be decent.** Assume the person on the other end is doing their best with limited time and
incomplete information — that applies to contributors, to me, and to anyone filing an issue.

**Keep it about the work.** Critique code, behaviour and decisions freely; that is useful.
Do not make it about the person.

**Respect other people's data.** Shopping lists are mundane but they are still someone's life.
Redact anything in an issue you would not want indexed by a search engine.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, or deliberately
wasting people's time.

If something here needs addressing, open a
[security advisory](https://github.com/SpellsPT/bring-mcp-catalog/security/advisories/new)
(it is private) or email the address on the commit history. I will deal with it as best I can,
which for a spare-time project means honestly rather than quickly.
